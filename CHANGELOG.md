# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Calculate directly in any amount field with `+`, `-`, `*`, and `/`. Conversions update as you type. Press `=` to replace the expression with its result.
- Calculations support localized amounts and spaces around operators. Invalid expressions keep the last valid conversion.
- See a one-time calculation example when you first focus an amount field. Full instructions are in Settings under Questions and answers.

## 0.1.0

- Initial release with single-pair and multiple-currency conversion.
